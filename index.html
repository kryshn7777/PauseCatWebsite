Pause Cat · a screen pet for Android

A cat for your home screen.

Pause Cat is a free screen pet for Android: a cartoon cat that lives on your home screen and walks onto apps like Instagram or TikTok when you stay too long. It never locks you out.

How it works

  1. Open your feed

    Pick the apps you lose time in and a limit: 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes. Then forget about it until the cat shows up.

  2. 1A cat steps in

    Stay past your limit and a cartoon cat walks onto your screen, right over the app.

  3. 2It gets cozier

    Stay longer and it sits on your feed, taps your screen, and finally naps on it.

  4. 2Then it naps

    "Leave app" and a snooze are always one tap away. It never locks you out.

  5. 3Leave, and it trots off

    Leave, and the cat leaves too, happy. Come back later and it might bring a little gift.

What it won't do

  • Lock you out.

    The app you're in keeps working, and "Leave app" and a snooze are always one tap away.

  • Get in your way.

    On your home screen it's see-through, so your taps go right through it.

  • Read your screen.

    It only checks which app is open and for how long, and that stays on your phone.

  • Ask for an account.

    You open it and pick your apps; there's nothing to sign up for.

  • Show ads or use AI.

    There are no ads and no AI features, and the extras are one-time purchases.

A pet on your screen

Your cat lives on your home screen. It strolls, stretches, makes biscuits and naps, and your taps go right through it. Turn see-through off and you can pet it. Go on, try it.

  • Pet it. It purrs.

    You feel a soft buzz. Tickle its belly when it flops over, or pick it up and it hangs on to your finger.

  • It lives on your home screen

    It's there most times you go home, and the widget says when it's napping. It also drops by in the apps you pick, at the times you pick. Call it any time from the widget.

  • Little gifts for time away

    Gifts, fish coins for rooms and toys, a diary of every moment you've seen, and outfits to unlock.

Questions

What is Pause Cat?

Pause Cat is a free screen pet for Android: a cartoon cat that lives on your home screen and walks onto apps like Instagram or TikTok when you stay too long. It never locks you out.

Is it like a desktop pet?

Yes, a virtual pet for your phone: a cartoon cat that lives on your home screen and walks over other apps. On your home screen it's see-through, so your taps go right through it.

Does Pause Cat block apps?

Pause Cat never locks you out: the cat gets in the way, playfully, and leaves when you leave, with "Leave app" and a snooze always one tap away.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app you pick: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, games, news.

How does it know when to show up?

With Android's usage access it checks which app is open, about once a second, only while the screen is on. It never sees what's on the screen.

Is it private?

No account, no ads, no AI features. Which apps you use, and for how long, stays on your phone. Purchases go through Google Play, whose billing library may send Google its own diagnostics. See the privacy policy.

Is it free?

Yes: the home-screen cat, watching your apps, every visit, gifts, the diary and Mango are free. More cats, outfits and treats are one-time purchases. No subscription.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your cat and your limits work without the internet; only purchases need Google Play.

Does it work on iPhone?

Pause Cat is Android only for now.

What permissions does it need?

Usage access (to know which app is open), display over other apps (to draw the cat), and notifications (for the small on-duty notification Android requires). The app explains each one before Android asks.

Can the cat visit when I haven't scrolled too long?

Yes, if you want: it can drop by to say hi in the places and at the times you pick, or never.
