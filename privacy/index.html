Pause Cat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Pause Cat is an Android app made by Snekanth BABU ("I", "me"). When you stay in an app you picked for longer than you wanted, a cartoon cat walks onto your screen and gently gets in the way until you leave. This policy explains what the app looks at, what it keeps, what leaves your phone, and what never happens.

The short version: Pause Cat checks which app is open and for how long, on your phone, to know when to send the cat. It never sees what's on your screen. There is no account, no ads, no analytics in the app and no AI features. What the app checks stays on your phone; I never receive it. Purchases go through Google Play. Pro's optional hold briefly catches taps on your screen and reads nothing.

Pause Cat does not read your screen content, notifications, messages, keystrokes, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not use an accessibility service.

When the cat holds the screen (Pro)

The hold is an optional Pro setting, on Android only. It is off until you turn it on. When it happens, the cat sits on the glass in one window over the app area only. That window catches your taps and swipes for up to 10 seconds, so the feed does not move. The status bar and navigation bar stay free, so Home and Back keep working. Leave app and Let go are on screen the whole time.

The hold does not read, record or keep your taps or what is behind the window. Nothing about it leaves your phone.

What leaves your phone

The app itself sends nothing to me. Only these things leave your phone, and only in these ways:

  • Purchases. Optional extras (more cats, outfits, packs, treats and Pro) are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's privacy policy; I never see your payment details. Google shows me order details in Google Play Console, such as the order number, the product, the price, its status and the country.
  • Google Play's billing library. The part of Google Play built into the app to handle purchases may send technical diagnostics (app performance and diagnostics data, such as connection problems and how purchase requests behave) to Google, under Google's privacy policy. It cannot be switched off, and I don't receive it.
  • Google Play's review prompt. If the app asks you to rate it, Google Play shows its own dialog. What you type there goes to Google Play, which publishes it as a review; the app never sees it.
  • Things you share. If you use Share (a video or GIF of your cat, or telling a friend), Android's share sheet sends it to the app you choose.
  • Reviews and emails. If you review Pause Cat on Google Play, Google publishes it and I can read and reply. If you email me, I receive your email address and message.

Pause Cat does not sell or share your personal information, and it shows no ads.

Backup and new phones

Pause Cat opts out of Android's cloud backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account. On a new phone your purchases come back through Google Play; everything else starts fresh.

This website

www.pausecat.com is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open it, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and the usual details a browser sends, and may keep them in logs for security, as any web host does. GitHub may process this in the USA; how it handles and protects that data is in GitHub's privacy statement. I need a host to put the site online (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app keeps lives in its private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall it, and it is gone. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you, then deleted. Purchase records are kept by Google, and by me in Google Play Console as long as tax and accounting law requires.
